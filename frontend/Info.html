Store Information

Paradigm Clothing Projects

Paradigm is an independent clothing label from the Philippines. We design graphic tees that mix street culture, anime, car culture and art, from original pieces to licensed-style collab tributes, released as seasonal archives and limited drops.

How we work

Every design starts as an original artwork. We print in small batches, so popular sizes can sell out. If something you like is gone, tap Notify Me on the item and we'll let you know when it's back.

Stay in the loop

Join the newsletter at the bottom of any page for drop dates, restocks and member-only codes.

Shipping

  • We ship nationwide within the Philippines.
  • Flat shipping fee: ₱150 per order.
  • Free shipping on orders of ₱2,000 or more (after discounts).
  • Orders are usually packed within 1–3 business days. Delivery takes about 2–5 business days in Metro Manila and 5–10 business days for provincial areas.
  • Once your order ships, the courier and tracking number appear on your My Orders page.
  • Pre-order items ship once restocked. We'll keep you updated on the order.

Payment

We currently accept Cash on Delivery. Please prepare the exact amount shown on your order. Online payment is coming soon.

Cancellations

You can cancel an order yourself from My Orders while it's still pending. Once it's paid or shipped, contact us instead.

Returns & exchanges

  • Request a return or exchange from My Orders within 30 days of your order being shipped.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and in their original condition with tags.
  • Size exchanges depend on stock. If your size is out, we'll offer a refund or another item.
  • Damaged, defective or wrong items: we cover return shipping. Please include photos in your request details.
  • Approved refunds for cash-on-delivery orders are sent by bank transfer or e-wallet (we'll ask for your details).

Send a transmission

Questions about an order, sizing or collabs? Message us and we'll reply by email. Include your order number if it's about an order.

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and handle personal data in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, phone number, optional profile picture, bio and size preference.
  • Order details: items, shipping address, contact number and delivery notes.
  • Your wishlist, reviews, restock requests and messages you send us.
  • Newsletter email, if you subscribe.
  • Basic, anonymous visit statistics (pages viewed) to improve the site.

How we use it

To process and deliver your orders, provide your account features, answer your messages, send updates you asked for, and prevent fraud. We share delivery details only with the courier handling your order. We never sell your data.

How we protect it

Passcodes are stored as one-way hashes, not plain text. Sessions use secure, HttpOnly cookies. Data is stored on encrypted cloud infrastructure, and only the store owner can access order and customer records.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you logged in, and your browser's local storage for your cart, theme and recently viewed items. We don't use advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can view and update your profile at any time, delete your account using "Reset Profile" on the login page, unsubscribe from the newsletter, or contact us to access, correct or delete your data. Order records may be kept as required for accounting.
